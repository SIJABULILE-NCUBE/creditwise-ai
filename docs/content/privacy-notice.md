# Privacy Notice

Last updated 27 Oct 2026

CreditWise AI helps you see your finances the way a lender does. To do that, we need some personal information. This notice explains what we collect, why, where it is kept, for how long, and how you can delete it. It follows the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

## What we collect

- **Your email address and first name,** when you create an account.
- **Your age confirmation and consent,** when you tick "I am 18 or older" and "I agree to the Privacy Notice".
- **Your income, expenses and debts,** when you set up and update your money profile.
- **Transactions you add,** with their date, description and amount, when you upload a CSV file or add one by hand.
- **Your questions to Ask CreditWise and its answers.**
- **Your checklist ticks** on the Get ready guides.

We do not ask for your ID number, bank account numbers or bank log-in details.

## Why we collect it

- Your email is used to log you in and to send verification and password reset emails.
- Your first name is used to greet you in the app.
- Your income, expenses and debts are used to calculate your debt-to-income ratio (DTI), Money left and Room.
- Your transactions are used to flag possible double charges, unusually high amounts and new payees.
- Your questions and figures are used to create answers in Ask CreditWise.
- Your checklist ticks are used to show your progress.

Your money details are confidential. We never use them for marketing, never sell or share them, and never show them to any other user.

## Where it's stored

Your information is stored with Supabase in London, United Kingdom.

When you ask Ask CreditWise a question, your question and your figures are sent to Anthropic in the United States to create the answer. Your name and email are never sent. Anthropic does not use these questions to train its models, and keeps them only as long as its API data policy allows.

Because your information is stored and processed outside South Africa, you agree to this when you tick "I agree to the Privacy Notice".

## How long we keep it

- **Account details, money profile and checklist ticks:** until you delete them or delete your account.
- **Transactions:** 90 days, then deleted automatically.
- **Ask CreditWise questions and answers:** 30 days, then deleted automatically.

## Your rights

You have the right to see, correct or delete your personal information:

- **See and correct:** all your information is shown in the app. Edit your income, debts and expenses on My Money.
- **Delete transactions:** Account, then "Delete all transactions".
- **Clear your chat:** Ask CreditWise, then "Clear chat".
- **Delete everything:** Account, then "Delete account". This permanently deletes your login and all your data straight away.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your information, you can complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa at inforegulator.org.za.
